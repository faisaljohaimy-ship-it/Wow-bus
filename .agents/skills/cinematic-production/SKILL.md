name: cinematic-production
description: Produce high-quality bilingual motion design and product films.
---

Specify storyboard, shot timing, typography, licensed media and audio. Select Remotion, Blender or FFmpeg after compatibility and license review. Render a short sample and check frame timing, clipping, audio and asset size.

For a first sample, compose the scene from implemented hub motions and render it with motion-library/render/render.mjs: it seeks frame by frame, so output is repeatable, and reports aspect ratio, frame rate, frame count, safe area, clipping, direction and contrast. Keep the film outside git and list what the report could not verify, such as audio and device playback.
